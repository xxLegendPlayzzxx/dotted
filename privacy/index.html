Privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026

In short

Dotted: eSign, Fill & Sign PDF is made by Dillon Pinto. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your documents, signatures and what you type stay on your device.

Your documents stay on your device

The PDFs you open, your signatures, what you type and the files you export are stored only in the app on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Deleting a document, or the app, removes them.

Your original files are never changed

When you open a file, the app makes its own copy. Exporting always writes a new PDF; the file you started from stays as it was.

Camera, photos and Face ID

The camera is used only while you scan a document or photograph your stamp. Photos are chosen with Apple's picker, so the app sees only the photos you pick. Face ID is checked by your device; the app never sees your face data.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. To confirm what you have bought, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase records. It does not receive your name, email, signatures or documents. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

Dotted is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
