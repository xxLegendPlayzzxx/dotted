Support

Answers to common questions about Dotted. If yours is not here, write to us.

How do I sign a document?

Open the document from Files, Mail or another app's share button, scan paper with Scan paper, or choose From Photos. Tap Sign, draw your signature once, then tap the page where it goes. Drag it to move it and use the corner handle to resize it. When you are done, tap Finish, then Make the PDF, and share it.

How do I fill in a form?

If the PDF has form fields, tap a field to type into it, or tap a checkbox or a choice. On a scan, or a form without fields, use Text, Date, Check and Cross, and tap where each one goes.

Is my original file changed?

No. Dotted keeps its own copy of what you open, and every export is a new PDF. The file you started from stays as it was.

Where are my documents kept?

In Dotted, on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting a document, or the app, removes them, so save the PDFs you want to keep to Files or share them.

Can I use these signatures on any document?

Signatures made in Dotted are electronic signatures. Many documents accept them, but some, such as wills or notarized papers, need more than an electronic signature. Check what your document requires; this page is not legal advice.

What is free, and what does Dotted Pro add?

Signing, filling in forms, text, dates, check marks and your first two documents are free, with no watermark, and exporting the same document again is always free. Dotted Pro adds unlimited documents and the Pro tools, such as several saved signatures, stamps, fonts, templates, merging pages and password-protected PDFs. A pack of 3 documents is also offered as a one-time purchase.

How do I restore my purchases?

In Dotted, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. The Dotted Pro screen has the same button. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel Dotted Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Dotted Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and choose the purchase.

I forgot the password of a PDF I locked.

Dotted does not keep the passwords you give exported PDFs, so they cannot be recovered. Your document is still in Dotted: make the PDF again with a new password.
